01 / Project Overview

Why environmental data quality matters

Environmental monitoring stations record rainfall, river-discharge and water-level measurements. Missing observations, duplicate timestamps and unusual readings can affect the reliability of environmental analysis. Our project investigated these issues and evaluated methods for identifying potential rainfall anomalies.

01 / DATA COLLECTION

Three sensor types

The dataset includes 113 rainfall files, 53 river-discharge files and 55 river-water-level files.

02 / DATA QUALITY

Statistical screening

The analysis examined missing measurements, duplicate timestamps and unusually high readings requiring further investigation.

03 / RESEARCH FOCUS

Rainfall anomaly detection

Four rainfall stations were selected for machine-learning evaluation following station screening and neighbour-gauge comparison.

Research scope: This system is a retrospective research prototype. The identified anomalies are not independently confirmed sensor failures, and the application is not a live operational warning service.
